  • Understanding Stablecoins: Uses, Technologies, and Incentives.
    Raphael Auer (Bank of International Settlements)
    Abstract. What are the economic uses cases for stablecoins, how do they differ from banks, and what are the implications of periodic departures from par? This talk offers a perspective grounded in evidence on their uses, technologies, and incentives. It first examines cross-border flows to understand transactional and speculative demand for stablecoins. It then stocktakes their technological architectures. Finally, it explores when making deposit-like claims tradable can be good for liquidity provision.
