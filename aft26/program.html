Program

Program · 6–9 October 2026 · London School of Economics. 36 papers in 12 sessions, preceded by the DeTaP workshop. Session chairs and the keynote title are to be announced.

2:00pm - 5:00pm DeTaP 2026 — 1st Workshop on Decentralisation: Theory and Practice
Organisers: Christina Ovezik and Mojtaba Tefagh (University of Edinburgh) · co-located with AFT 2026
  • Invited and contributed talks, with dedicated time for open discussion
    Defining and measuring decentralisation; governance; legal tests and liability; trade-offs against efficiency, scalability and accountability; case studies and comparisons with other fields
  • Cross-disciplinary participation from cryptography, distributed systems, economics and law
    No proceedings: work in progress and work published or submitted elsewhere are equally welcome
  • Free to attend; separate registration required as places are limited
    blockchain-technology-lab.github.io/DeTaP
